About

I'm finishing a BS in Computer Engineering and Computer Science at UW–Madison in summer 2027 and starting an MS that fall, focused on ASIC/FPGA design. In summer 2027 I'll join Leonardo DRS as a Senior Technical Intern in power electronics.

Optically isolated GaN gate drive

Undergraduate Researcher, Wide-Bandgap Materials and Devices Lab

A discrete, chip-on-board gate drive that switches bare-die GaN HEMTs through optical fiber, targeting sub-10 ns VDS transitions with full galvanic isolation. I modeled GaAs phototransistor switching in LTspice, laid out the chip-on-board PCBs in Altium to minimize parasitic inductance, and evaluated switching speed with custom-packaged bare-die parts in a double-pulse test.

I presented this work at the 2026 ECE SPARK poster symposium and received the Complex Concepts Communicator Award, one of three awards across 31 presenters, for clarity in explaining the research to peers and faculty.

Three-channel tunable GaN laser driver

Undergraduate Researcher, Wide-Bandgap Materials and Devices Lab

A 4-layer Altium board, paired with a step-down power board, that sets 0.2–2.5 A per channel from a single voltage bus. It removed three external current generators and six input ports from the test setup, and it replicated prior results on a far smaller board than the off-the-shelf EPC drivers it replaced.

Optically powered GaN gate drive In progress

Undergraduate Researcher, Wide-Bandgap Materials and Devices Lab

The next step removes the electrical supply from the gate side entirely. A sandwich PCB holds two OSRAM hyper-red LEDs about 8 mm above an ANYSOLAR SM500K12L PV cell. The PV cell, with parallel capacitance, supplies the gate-drive rail, and a totem pole of custom PAAS phototransistors drives the gate of an Innoscience INN650N080BS bare-die GaN FET.

  • Optical power delivery
  • Bare-die GaN
  • Sandwich PCB

Pipelined RISC-V CPU

Academic project, team of three

A 5-stage pipelined RV32I processor in Verilog. Data hazards are handled with EX/MEM and MEM/WB forwarding plus a one-cycle stall on load-use. Branches are predicted not taken and resolved in EX, with a pipeline flush on redirect.

  • Verilog
  • RV32I
  • Data forwarding
  • Hazard detection
  • Branch prediction

      Solar Racing: lighting control board

      Low Voltage Team Member, Badger Solar Racing

      Problem
      6 of 7 first-build boards failed when the 12 V header shorted to the adjacent 5 V test header.
      Fix
      I separated the headers and added an eFuse and overvoltage protection in the Altium redesign, then hand-assembled and soldered the new boards.
      Result
      The redesign ran failure-free through several full-vehicle trial assemblies and passed the road safety check.

      I also worked with the firmware team on the CAN protocol that links the board to the rest of the car.

      • eFuse and OVP
      • CAN bus
